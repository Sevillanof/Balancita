## Objective

Build a deterministic, isolated paper-futures path that can take public Kraken market observations through analysis, risk, paper order, fill, position, close, and complete USD net accounting. It must support reproducible streaming and replay while leaving existing spot/synthetic behavior and history intact. Automated paper trades are explicitly in scope; real trades are not. Completion means a working end-to-end simulation with evidence, not merely a contracts-only scaffold.

## Problem and why

The current BTC-EUR intelligence and synthetic simulation path is not a USD-settled futures simulator. Reusing its floating-point spot assumptions, silently changing historic economics, or labeling predicted funding as realized would produce misleading results. A separate, versioned futures path can test the full lifecycle deterministically without authorizing trading or changing historical results.

## Resolved engineering contract

### Determinism, IPC, and durable acceptance

- JSONL protocol version 1 includes an explicit acknowledgement `type` (an intentional addition to the guide). Bind messages to `protocol_version`, `request_id`, `run_id`, `applied_state_version`, and `result_hash` where applicable.
- The internal Python acknowledgement with status `committed` is emitted only after Node's single atomic SQLite transaction commits the applied work and its result/events/ledger/projections/checkpoint/outbox. The external UI `command.ack` means durable command acceptance: emit it after the command-acceptance row and its acceptance-outbox event commit, without waiting for simulation completion. The later `command.result` is persisted and emitted in its own durable transaction. These acknowledgements represent distinct boundaries.
- Retries retain a stable job ID. Repeating an identical payload returns the stored effect; reusing an ID with a different payload rejects. A stale expected version is superseded, never applied to newer state.
- Canonical hashing: recursively sorted object keys; arrays retain semantic order; plain normalized decimal strings without exponent; normalize negative zero to `0`; timestamps are UTC integer milliseconds; reject NaN, Infinity, and undefined. Publish cross-language hash vectors and verify them in Node and Python.
- Feed sequence tracking is isolated by feed/product/epoch. A gap conservatively invalidates the affected state and requires resnapshot/reconciliation before trading decisions. Provider sequence guarantees are not proven until fixture/source evidence establishes them.

### Money, position, fees, and funding

- Futures accounting uses BTC quantity, USD/BTC prices, and USD settlement. Use Decimal throughout new financial logic; serialize normalized decimal strings. Never perform financial math through JavaScript `Number`.
- Preserve exact internal amounts; do not claim exchange rounding parity. Cents are display-only. Partial-close allocations are proportional, with any residual assigned to the final allocation.
- New cost identity: `kraken-futures-eea-btcusd-base.v1`, maker `0.0002`, taker `0.0005`, lab configuration only—not a user's account-tier claim. Verify against official fee documentation before implementation. Example excluding funding: long gross `10`, fees `1.005`, net `8.995`; short gross `10`, fees `.995`, net `9.005`.
- Funding availability and funding amount are distinct: unknown funding is not zero and makes net P&L incomplete. Normalize the observed current rate as absolute USD/BTC/hour with explicit provider semantics, and accrue deterministically as rate × elapsed time × position quantity using UTC time and the effective interval. Accrual must be segmented at hourly/interval and position-change boundaries, not charged once per analysis. An unknown effective interval makes funding/net incomplete. Predicted rates are not actual rates; ambiguous `next_funding_rate_time` semantics must never drive accrual.
- Require a current versioned instrument catalog before admitting real-data instruments to `paper_live`; metadata mismatch blocks `paper_live` activation/entries, not offline ledger implementation or fixture replay. For current EEA BTCUSD contract assumptions, minimum quantity is `0.0001 BTC` and tick `1 USD`; mismatch fails closed. Confirm catalog and instrument metadata before `paper_live` activation; these values are not asserted as live-verified here.
- Replay assigns new run/analysis IDs. Compare economic/semantic canonical outputs while excluding generated IDs and actual execution-time fields; do not require byte-identical full records or hashes across distinct runs.
- Futures-specific work must preserve existing C25-C28 rules, diagnostics, strategy ownership, and regime behavior; it does not reopen those product decisions. New futures IDs remain isolated as `*-perp-v1`.
